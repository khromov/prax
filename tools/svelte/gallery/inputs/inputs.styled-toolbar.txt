# The toolbar of the styled text area. A style button gives what the area's selection holds its style, or with nothing
# selected, the style of what is typed next at the caret. Undo and Redo step the history of the area's buffer. Each
# button returns focus to the area, with its selection as it stood, so that typing goes on. The example does these
# through the area's view (prUiTextEditView, prTextEditViewSetStyle, prTextEditHistoryUndo, prUiSetFocus), and the port
# asks the area's node for them with `style-selection`, `undo`, `redo` and `focus`.
#
